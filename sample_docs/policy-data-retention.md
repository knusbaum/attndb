# Data Retention Policy

## Status

Approved. Effective this fiscal year. Owner: Legal and Data Governance.

## Philosophy

We retain personal data only as long as it serves the purpose for which it was
collected. When a purpose lapses, the data is a liability rather than an asset, so
the default posture is deletion. Teams must justify retention, not deletion.

## Retention Periods

Operational logs are kept for thirty days. Customer account records are retained
for the life of the account plus ninety days. Financial records are held for seven
years to satisfy regulatory obligations. Anything not covered by a specific
schedule defaults to ninety days.

## Deletion and Erasure

On account closure, customer records enter a ninety-day grace window and are then
purged from primary stores and backups. A verified erasure request is honored
within thirty days across all systems, including analytics warehouses.

## Exceptions

A legal hold overrides any retention schedule. Data under hold is preserved until
the hold is lifted by the Legal team, regardless of its normal expiry.
